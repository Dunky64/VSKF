# A business phone number that rings your mobile

You don't need this until about February 2027: the photo-quote form is the main way in,
and the website works without a phone number. **Every option below costs money, so it's
your call.** Prices change, so check each provider's website before you sign up.

## Option 1: a local 01484 number that forwards to your mobile ("virtual landline")

Looks the most established for a local business. Typically a few pounds a month, sometimes
plus a per-minute charge for forwarding calls. UK providers that offer this include
Sipgate, Hello Telecom, Number Supermarket, Yay.com and Vonage (not an endorsement, compare them).

Steps:
1. Pick a provider and choose a monthly plan with **call forwarding to a mobile**.
2. Choose a **01484** (Huddersfield) number. Some let you pick an easy-to-remember one.
3. In their app or website, set **forward calls to** your mobile number.
4. Turn on **show the caller's number** (so you see who's calling, not the 01484 number).
   If they offer it, set a **whisper** message like "Driveway Butler call", so you know
   to answer professionally.
5. Record a short **voicemail greeting**: "You've reached The Driveway Butler. Leave your
   name and postcode, or get an instant quote at [website]."
6. Optional: set **business hours**, so calls outside them go straight to voicemail.
7. **Test it:** call the 01484 number from a friend's phone. Check it rings your mobile and
   that voicemail works.
8. Tell me the number and I'll add it to the website.

Check whether the plan lets you **call out** showing the 01484 number. Not all do. If not,
customers will see your mobile number when you call them back.

## Option 2: a second mobile number on your phone (often cheapest)

Most recent phones take two SIMs (one can be an eSIM). Get a cheap SIM-only or
pay-as-you-go deal and add it as a second line. It gives you a separate 07 number, a different
ringtone for business calls, and you can call out on it. Downsides: it's an 07 number,
not a local one.

## My recommendation

Wait until about February 2027. If the budget allows then, go with **Option 1**
(a local number builds trust on leaflets). Until then, the website will show the quote form only.
